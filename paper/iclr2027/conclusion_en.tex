We introduced Block3 Mean, an on-policy distillation update that combines shared feedback over adjacent tokens with a joint block ratio and valid-length weighting. The method reuses teacher scores while changing the unit through which they influence the student. Experiments show substantial gains in the historical Qwen3-1.7B setting and positive final Avg@8 differences for the official 8B-to-1.7B Instruct pair, alongside checkpoint reversals and settings that favor token-level updates. The analysis explains how the objective introduces cross-token credit, scale changes and a different clipping geometry. Together with the retained trajectory diagnostics, these results identify local credit assignment as a concrete design choice for reasoning distillation and provide a basis for separating its optimization components.
