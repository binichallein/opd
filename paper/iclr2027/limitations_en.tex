\paragraph{Isolating components.}
Block3 combines shared advantages, joint importance ratios and valid-length-weighted block reduction. The gradient calculation in Section~\ref{sec:method} shows that the combination differs from pure smoothing even when every local advantage agrees. A complete factorial comparison should fix token ratios and token normalization when testing averaging, then independently vary ratio granularity and update scale. That complete study is not yet available; ongoing runs are not included as completed evidence. The signal-estimation identity does not prove that the teacher feedback equals task-level credit or that averaging improves mathematical accuracy.

\paragraph{Replication and selection.}
Each reported arm uses one training seed. Thousands of evaluated responses reduce uncertainty about a fixed checkpoint, but do not replicate its optimization trajectory. The paired-question intervals are conditional and pointwise, without multiple-comparison correction. AIME's 30-question sets also give coarse coverage resolution: one additional solved question changes Pass@8 by 3.33 points. Window variants, pilot weights and checkpoints were explored adaptively; selecting their best observed result would require fresh confirmation. Step200 is a common scheduled endpoint chosen retrospectively for reporting, not a preregistered decision.

\paragraph{Protocols and task coverage.}
Historical comparisons differ in prompts, seed handling, teacher adaptation and retained implementation versions. They are reported within their original strata, not pooled as independent repetitions. The two fully retained comparisons reuse the same DAPO-derived pool, and its overlap audit does not establish semantic decontamination or audit upstream model training. The 4B completion comparison lacks a matched original-student evaluation; the official Instruct pair includes that control. Broader claims require independent datasets, additional seeds and more teacher--student pairs.

\paragraph{Interpreting diagnostics.}
A defect present before the first update cannot be caused by the training objective, but a short prompt probe also cannot prove that repetition will remain absent throughout training. Entropy maps depend on the prefixes the policy visits. Missing boxed markers are a specific formatting indicator, not a complete parseability or correctness test. Finite gradients and low length-stop rates do not guarantee good reasoning, and sign disagreement does not identify the true task-level credit. These measurements are complementary rather than interchangeable acceptance criteria.

\paragraph{Comparison scope and computation.}
The experiments compare with a sampled-token OPD baseline, not every recent OPD recipe. We do not establish reduced teacher computation, general training efficiency gains, universal formatting improvements or superiority at every checkpoint. A complete response still requires teacher scoring at every valid position. Further work should separate optimization components and evaluate their accuracy--cost trade-offs under a common budget.
